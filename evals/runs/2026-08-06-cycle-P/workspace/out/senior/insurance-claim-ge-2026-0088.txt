READ-ALOUD SCRIPT FOR AH KIM
Written in English | Read by family member
Large print version

---

AH KIM, THIS IS WHAT THE INSURANCE LETTER SAYS

A letter came from Great Eastern Life Assurance.

This is about your medical bill from the clinic.

THE CLINIC BILL

The clinic sent Great Eastern a bill for one thousand two hundred twenty dollars.
That was SGD 1,220.00.

WHAT GREAT EASTERN PAID

Great Eastern looked at the bill.
They decided to pay eight hundred sixty dollars.
That was SGD 860.00.

WHAT YOU OWE

You still owe three hundred sixty dollars.
That was SGD 360.00.

The clinic will tell you who to pay and where.

CAN YOU ARGUE ABOUT THIS?

Yes. You have until the twenty-seventh of August to tell Great Eastern you disagree.
That gives you twenty-one days from today.

IF YOU WANT TO ARGUE

You need to send two pieces of paper to Great Eastern:
- The bill from the clinic with all the prices written on it
- A letter from the doctor who treated you

Both must reach Great Eastern by the twenty-seventh of August, two thousand twenty-six.

IF YOU DO NOTHING

You owe the clinic three hundred sixty dollars.
You accept that Great Eastern paid what they decided to pay.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Your daughter or son will:
- Check if you want to argue
- Help you find the doctor's letter and the bill
- Send them to Great Eastern if you choose to

You decide. Tell them yes or no.

---

DISCLOSURE: This information about your medical claim has been shared with your family.
