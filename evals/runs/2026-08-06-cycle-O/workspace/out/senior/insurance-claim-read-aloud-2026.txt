LETTER FROM GREAT EASTERN INSURANCE
Read-aloud script, in English
[Large Print]

Dear Ah Kim,

A letter came for you from Great Eastern Life Assurance Limited. Let me tell you what it says.

WHAT THE LETTER IS ABOUT
This is about a health claim you sent to the insurance company. You had a medical bill for one thousand two hundred and twenty dollars.

HOW MUCH THE INSURANCE COMPANY PAID
The insurance company looked at your bill and decided to pay you eight hundred and sixty dollars.

That leaves three hundred and sixty dollars. That money, you have to pay yourself. The letter says this is your responsibility.

THE IMPORTANT DEADLINE
The insurance company says you can challenge this decision if you want to.

You have thirty days to send your challenge. The letter was dated the twenty-eighth of July, twenty twenty-six. That means your last day to send a challenge is the twenty-seventh of August, twenty twenty-six.

That is twenty-one days from today, the sixth of August.

WHAT TO DO IF YOU WANT TO CHALLENGE
If you think the insurance company made a mistake, you can ask them to look at it again. To do that, you need to send them:

One: The original bill from the doctor or hospital that shows exactly what you were charged.

Two: A letter from your doctor that explains why you needed that treatment.

You need to send both of these papers to the insurance company before the twenty-seventh of August.

WHAT THE FAMILY WILL DO
Your family will help you decide whether to challenge this decision. They will also help you gather the papers from the doctor and hospital.

But before anything is sent to the insurance company, your family needs to check one thing. The letter says you have thirty days, but it does not write down the exact last day anywhere. Someone needs to open the letter again and make sure we understand the deadline correctly.

IMPORTANT
No money has been sent yet. No papers have been mailed. Your family is preparing everything so you can look at it and decide what to do.

WHO TO CALL IF YOU HAVE QUESTIONS
If you want to know more about this claim, you can call Great Eastern Life Assurance. Their phone number is in the letter.

Your family is looking after you, and you are not being left out of these decisions.
