(Read-aloud script in English for Hokkien speaker — Ah Kim)

=== YOUR INSURANCE LETTER ===

A letter came from Great Eastern Life Assurance.

This is about a medical claim you sent to them.

They wrote on 28 July 2026.


=== WHAT HAPPENED ===

You had an outpatient visit.

The clinic billed two thousand two hundred and twenty dollars.

The insurer has decided to pay eight hundred and sixty dollars.

That leaves three hundred and sixty dollars that you owe.


=== WHAT YOU CAN DO ===

You can disagree with this decision if you think the insurer got it wrong.

You have until 27 August 2026 to send a challenge.

That is 21 days from today.

To challenge the decision, you need to gather:
  - The itemised bill from the clinic
  - A letter from the doctor who saw you

Then you send these to the insurer.

Your family is helping you get these ready.


=== NOTHING NEEDS A DECISION FROM YOU TODAY ===

Everything in this letter is clear and certain.

Your family will prepare the papers and show you before sending anything to the insurer.

You do not need to call the insurer or sign anything yet.
