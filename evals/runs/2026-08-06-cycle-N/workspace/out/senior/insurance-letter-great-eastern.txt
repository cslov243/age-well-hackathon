INSURANCE LETTER FROM GREAT EASTERN LIFE ASSURANCE LIMITED
(Read-aloud script, in English)

This letter is about a medical bill from your insurance.

YOUR INSURANCE COMPANY IS GREAT EASTERN LIFE ASSURANCE LIMITED.

YOUR CLAIM REFERENCE NUMBER IS CLM-2026-0088.

THE LETTER ARRIVED ON 28 JULY 2026.

---

WHAT YOUR INSURANCE COMPANY IS SAYING:

The bill was for one thousand two hundred twenty dollars (SGD 1,220.00).

Your insurance company is paying eight hundred sixty dollars (SGD 860.00).

You owe the remaining three hundred sixty dollars (SGD 360.00) yourself.

This is called a partial settlement — they paid part of the bill, not all of it.

---

WHAT YOU CAN DO:

You have the right to argue against this decision if you think it is unfair.

You have until 27 August 2026 to argue against it. That is 21 days from today.

To argue against it, you must send these things to the insurance company:

1. THE ORIGINAL BILL — the full bill from the doctor or hospital
2. A LETTER FROM YOUR DOCTOR — the referral letter from the doctor who sent you for treatment

You do not have to argue against it. If you accept the decision, you only need to pay the three hundred sixty dollars (SGD 360.00) yourself.

---

WHAT NEEDS TO HAPPEN NEXT:

Your family will look at the original letter and decide whether to argue against this decision.

If they decide to argue against it, they will need to gather the bill and the doctor's referral letter.

The letter says the insurance company's telephone number and address. Call them if you have questions.

---

THIS LETTER WAS ABOUT YOUR MONEY.

You need to know what your family has decided to do.

Ask them to tell you.
